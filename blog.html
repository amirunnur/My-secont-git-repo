1.Simple refreshing facial wash

Overview: A gentle, soap-free gel cleanser designed for daily use on all skin types, including sensitive skin.

Ingredients:

  • Surfactant: cocamidopropyl betaine — mild cleanser
  • Soothing: panthenol (pro-vitamin B5) & allantoin
  • Antioxidant: vitamin E

Pros:

  • Free from fragrance, parabens, alcohol, and harsh chemicals — ideal for sensitive or reactive skin
  • Non-comedogenic and dermatologically approved

Summary: A reliable, minimalist cleanser suitable for everyday use.

2.Simple Hydrating Light Moisturiser

Overview: A lightweight, fragrance-free moisturizer formulated to hydrate without greasiness. Ideal for normal to combination skin.

Pros:

  • Minimal ingredients reduce risk of irritation
  • Provides balanced hydration, likely containing humectants like glycerin/propylene glycol

Summary: Great for daily use, especially under sunscreen or makeup

3.Omi Brotherhood Menthol Gel Sunscreen

Overview: A cooling gel sunscreen containing menthol, popular in some Asian skincare routines.

Pros:

  • Cooling effect provides refreshing feel in hot, humid climates
  • Gel texture absorbs quickly without stickiness

Summary: Great if you like cooling sunscreens and your skin tolerates menthol.

4.Garnier Micellar Water

Overview: Iconic micellar cleanser for face, eyes, and lips—no rinsing required. Affordable and widely available.

Pros:

  • Efficient at removing makeup (up to 90% in one swipe)
  • Gentle, fragrance-free, suitable for most skin types

Cons:

  • May not remove heavy/waterproof makeup completely
  • Some users report oily residue, dryness, irritation, or breakout

Summary: A budget-friendly, convenient makeup remover—but patch testing is advised, especially for sensitive or oily/acne-prone skin.
